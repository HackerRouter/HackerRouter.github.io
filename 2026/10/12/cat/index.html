Cat

A cat. A black cat. A black cat with yellow eyes.

Sitting right in front of me.

“Meow.”

People around me couldn’t stop talking about how adorable it was.

Maybe it had a name. Maybe not.

I turned towards the stairs.

And it turned towards me.

Welp.

I turned away, slowly this time, and went upstairs.

Hours later, I stepped out of my dorm room.

There it was.

Just around the corner.

“Meow.”

It looked up at me, as if we’d never parted.

I went to get some water. On my way back, I noticed a little shadow trailing behind me.

Black fur. Yellow eyes.

Still that same cat.

What do you do when a cat decides to follow you?

I almost wanted to Google it.

“Guess you like me, huh?”

I crouched down to meet its eyes.

“Meow.”

And another.

And another.

“Well, I’ve got nothing to feed you.”

I shrugged, stood up, and headed back to my room.

Little footsteps followed.

And so did the meowing.

“Sorry. I can’t let you in.”

I eased the door shut, leaving only a narrow gap between us.

“Meow.”

Still there.

“Meow.”

I stood on the other side, listening.

For a while, there was nothing but its voice.

Then, nothing at all.

I waited a little longer before opening the door a crack.

No black fur.

No yellow eyes.

Just an empty hallway.

I don’t know why that made me sad.

Maybe it had somewhere else to go.

Maybe it didn’t.

I wish I could be a cat, too.

Just anything but myself.
