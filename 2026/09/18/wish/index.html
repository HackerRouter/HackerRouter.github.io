Wish

Sometimes they called me a genius.

Sometimes they said I was talented.

But I wish I could just be normal.

Or maybe never have been born at all.

I wish I could just be a normal person, living a normal life.

I wouldn’t need to do anything great.
I wouldn’t need to carry everyone’s expectations.
I wouldn’t need to change my body.
I wouldn’t need to change my mind.

I wouldn’t need to face all that hatred and malice just because I’m different.

I wouldn’t need to deal with all those exhausting formalities in the future just to prove who I am.

I wouldn’t need to be ostracized.
No. No. No. No.

And I wouldn’t have to know so many things.

I wouldn’t have to walk down some completely different road in life.

I wouldn’t have to achieve anything.

I wouldn’t have to make a name for myself in some field.

I could just exist.

Just live.

And maybe that would have been enough.

Jesus.

Does depression ever really go away?
