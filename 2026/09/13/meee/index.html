Side B of Me IRL

Defective. Clumsy. “Mentally ill.” Freak.

And being reminded of those things every second of every day of every year, in real life.

Even after I’ve escaped the environment I used to be trapped in?

Every time I think about the bullying, the exclusion, and the discrimination I went through, it still makes me sick.

I still feel like throwing up.

Ironically, even today, even though I’m fucking grown up—

my parents still won’t, or aren’t willing to, understand what it feels like to be constantly reminded of those things.

They are part of those reminders. Of course they are.

And I guess they think I don’t deserve to have thoughts of my own.

And yes. Even today. Even though I’m fucking grown up.

I guess that’s it.

When I was younger, I would look at the deformity in the mirror with disgust.

and they said you worry too much.

they said it’ll fix itself once you fucking grow up.

why bother seeing a fucking doctor. why waste the money.

but it didn’t.

and now i see a doctor.

and the doctor tells me that once you’ve grown up, it’s already too late to fix it.

and they say that’s my fault.

I don’t actually know what I’m talking about anymore.

I don’t even know what I’m typing down.

I guess I just have to endure all the torture, all the bad things, all the bad experiences, all the bad thoughts on my own.

Always on my own.

I don’t expect anyone to ever understand me.

No expectations. No disappointment.

Hell is other people.

For years.

For as long as I can remember.

Can I become the person I’ve always wanted to be?

It’s so hard…

But I have to.

I have to become that person.

And I have to keep going.
